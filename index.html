Our story

Two paths. One thoughtful idea.

The Self-Care Shift was inspired by the combination of Ava Guerrieri’s interest in entering the healthcare field and Miranda Hamilton’s hairstyling background.

Together, we imagined a way to bring convenient hair care to the people who spend their days caring for others. Our goal is to make a little self-care easier to fit into a demanding schedule.

For hospitals and care facilities

Bring a little self-care to your team.

We envision partnering with hospitals and healthcare facilities to schedule on-site salon visits and explore employee-sponsored appointments.
